# Refund Policy

## Timeline
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 working days after the returned item passes quality check.
- Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method.
- Cash on delivery orders are refunded to the bank account shared by the customer.

## Refund limits
- Refunds above Rs 5000 need manual approval from a human agent.
- Shipping charges are refunded only if the item was damaged or wrong.

## Cancelled orders
- Cancelled prepaid orders are refunded within 3-5 working days.
