# Cancellation Policy

## When cancellation is allowed
- An order can be cancelled only while its status is "placed" (before shipping).
- Once an order is "shipped", it cannot be cancelled. The customer must wait for delivery and then use the return process.

## Already cancelled orders
- A cancelled order cannot be cancelled again or reactivated. The customer must place a new order.

## Refund after cancellation
- Prepaid orders are refunded within 3-5 working days.
