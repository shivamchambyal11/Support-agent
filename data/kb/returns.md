# Return Policy

## Eligibility
- Standard return window: 7 days from the delivery date.
- Gold tier customers get 10 days. Premium tier customers get 14 days.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.

## Damaged or wrong items
- If the item is damaged or wrong, return is allowed within 15 days of delivery for all tiers.
- Customer must share a photo of the item.

## Non-returnable items
- Innerwear, perishable goods and customized products cannot be returned.

## How to return
- Return is initiated from the order page. Pickup is arranged within 2 working days.
